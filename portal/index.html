Does the rewritten alert still say what the official alert said?

Paste an official flood or landslide alert and a rewritten version: an SMS, a radio script, a translation, or an AI's simplification. Sachet checks every level, time, place and instruction, and shows what was kept, lost, invented or flipped.

🔒 Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you paste is uploaded or stored.

What Sachet checks

  • Numbers, times, dates: water levels, thresholds, times, the 1155 hotline. Devanagari and Latin digits count as equal (६.८ = 6.8).
  • Places: the names you list above, in any of the spellings you give.
  • Meaning: the hazard, the status (above or below the warning or danger level), the trend (rising or falling) and the action (move to high ground, keep away from the river).
  • Nothing invented or flipped: no new numbers, and "above" never becomes "below".

What Sachet does not do

  • It does not confirm that an alert is official. Check the issuing agency: DHM, BIPAD Portal, NDRRMA. Flood information: 1155.
  • A clean result means "no missing, invented or flipped facts found", not "safe to broadcast". A person should always review a message before it goes out.
  • Checks are rule-based and still being validated. Unusual wording can be missed.
